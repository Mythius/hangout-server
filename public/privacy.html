Privacy Policy

Last updated July 31, 2026

Hangout is a simple app for letting friends know when you're free to hang out. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how to request that it be deleted.

Information we collect

  • Google account info — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Phone number — you provide this yourself so friends can find and add you.
  • Friends — who you've sent or received friend requests with, and your accepted connections.
  • Availability status — whether you've marked yourself available to hang out, and the optional custom message you write for friends to see when you do.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used only to deliver notifications to your device via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and there's no advertising or analytics tracking in the app.

How we use this information

Strictly to run the app's features: signing you in, letting friends find you by phone number, showing your accepted friends who's available, and sending a notification when a friend you've connected with becomes available. We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Sharing

Your phone number and availability are only ever visible to friends you've mutually accepted a connection with — never to other users, and never publicly. The only outside parties involved are Google (for sign-in) and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver push notifications) — both strictly as infrastructure to run the app, not for their own use.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data (profile, phone number, friend connections, and availability history), email southwickmatthias@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Hangout isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "last updated" date.
